JACKPOT — born to mangle
=======================

INSTALL
  1. Quit Ableton.
  2. Double-click "Install JACKPOT.pkg" and follow the steps.
  3. Open Ableton > Settings > Plug-Ins, turn on "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders", click Rescan.
  4. Plug-Ins > Jackpot Audio > JACKPOT. Drop it on your guitar track.

UNINSTALL
  Double-click "Uninstall JACKPOT". It asks whether to keep your saved patches
  (Documents/JACKPOT) and asks for your Mac password to remove the plug-ins.

IF macOS SAYS IT CAN'T OPEN THE INSTALLER
  JACKPOT isn't signed by an Apple-registered developer, so macOS is cautious.
  - macOS 15 (Sequoia) and newer: try to open it once, then go to
    System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway".
  - Older macOS: right-click (or Control-click) the file and choose Open, then Open again.
  Do the same for "Uninstall JACKPOT" the first time you use it.

Works on macOS 10.15 or newer, Apple Silicon and Intel.
