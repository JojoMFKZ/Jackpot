ODDKIN is installed.

  1. Open Ableton Live → Settings → Plug-Ins.
  2. Turn on Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders, then click Rescan.
  3. Find it under Plug-Ins → Oddkin → ODDKIN and drop it on your guitar track.

Your saved patches go to Documents/ODDKIN/Presets.

To remove ODDKIN later, open Applications → Oddkin → Uninstall ODDKIN.
