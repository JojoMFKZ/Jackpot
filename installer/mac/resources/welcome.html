JACKPOT

Jackpot Audio

This installs the JACKPOT guitar sound explorer for Ableton Live and other music apps:

  • VST3 → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 (Ableton uses this one)
  • Audio Unit → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components (Logic, GarageBand)

Click Customize on the next step if you only want one of them.

Quit Ableton before installing.
