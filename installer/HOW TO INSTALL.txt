ODDKIN
=======
A guitar effect that helps you find weird, unique sounds.
Wander an endless map, breed sounds like little creatures, keep the ones you love.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MAC  (macOS 10.15 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Quit Ableton.
2. Double-click  ODDKIN-Installer-Mac.pkg  and click through.
3. If macOS says it can't open it (the plug-in isn't signed by Apple):
     macOS 15+:  try once, then System Settings > Privacy & Security >
                 scroll down > "Open Anyway".
     Older:      right-click the file > Open > Open.
4. To uninstall later: Applications > Oddkin > Uninstall ODDKIN.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WINDOWS  (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Quit Ableton.
2. Double-click  ODDKIN-Installer-Windows.exe  and click through.
3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info" > "Run anyway".
4. To uninstall later: Settings > Apps > ODDKIN (or Start menu > ODDKIN).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IN ABLETON LIVE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Settings/Preferences > Plug-Ins > turn on "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders"
> Rescan. Then drag  Plug-Ins > Oddkin > ODDKIN  onto your guitar track.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- MAP: click or drag anywhere. Every spot is a different sound; nearby spots
  sound alike. "Teleport" jumps to a brand-new region. Close / Wider / Wild
  sets how fast things change as you move.
- FAMILY: the creature is your current sound. Its six kids are variations -
  click one to make it the new parent. "Breed new litter" deals new kids.
- MATES: press "Keep" on sounds you like. Kept sounds can be picked as a mate,
  and the kids inherit traits from both parents.
- TRAITS: each trait is an effect (spiky hair = fuzz, halo = reverb, ghost
  trail = delay, third eye = stutter, antennae = harmony, heart = resonator...).
  Click a trait to lock it and every kid inherits it.
- MELODIC STUFF: Harmony adds chord voices. Resonator rings in a key - set
  KEY under the hood to your song's key and it stays there.
- UNDER THE HOOD: every real knob, if you ever want them.
- TRAIL: every sound you've visited. Click one to go back.
- Map X and Map Y can be automated in Ableton, so your sound can wander
  across the map during a song.

Kept and saved sounds live in Documents/ODDKIN/Presets.
