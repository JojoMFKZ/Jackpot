ODDKIN - born to mangle
=======================

USING IT IN ABLETON
  Options > Preferences > Plug-Ins > turn on "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders" > Rescan.
  Then: Plug-Ins > Oddkin > ODDKIN. Drop it on your guitar track.

WHERE THINGS ARE
  Plug-in:        C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\ODDKIN.vst3
  Saved patches:  Documents\ODDKIN\Presets

UNINSTALL
  Settings > Apps > ODDKIN > Uninstall
  (or Start menu > ODDKIN > Uninstall ODDKIN)
  It asks whether to delete your saved patches too.

IF WINDOWS SAYS "WINDOWS PROTECTED YOUR PC"
  The installer isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen is cautious.
  Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
